DESCRIPTION

yourname studies computer science at McMaster University and is most at home in the layers people rarely see: operating systems, networks, and the infrastructure everything else runs on. That interest started in 2022 with a Raspberry Pi running two services, and the homelab is now on its third version. The plan is a career in security or infrastructure, ideally where the two meet, with plenty of curiosity left over for the rest of the field.

yourname daily-drives Fedora Linux and GrapheneOS and lives as de-Googled a life as possible: free and open-source software first, offline tools where the task allows, and self-hosted services for the rest. The software people depend on should be free to read, change and share.

OPTIONS

--security
How systems fail, and how to make failure boring. Threat modeling, hardening, and learning to think like an attacker.
--infrastructure
Linux, networking, virtualization, and the plumbing that keeps services up: reverse proxies, DNS, monitoring and backups. Most of it learned by running a homelab.
--free-software
Tools that can be read, audited and fixed, preferably ones that keep working offline. Most of the code lives on Codeberg.
--privacy
Collect less and encrypt the rest. A de-Googled phone on GrapheneOS, self-hosted services instead of rented ones, and a threat model for everything else.
